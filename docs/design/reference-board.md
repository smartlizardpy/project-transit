# Reference board: City Transport Manager press kit

All images are hosted by the publisher; links only, nothing copied into this repo. Base: https://citytransportmanager.com/presskit/files-screens/ (iPad, iPhone, UI-English folders) and /presskit/files-logo/ for branding. File names below are given as folder/name.png. Treat everything as inspiration only. Neither "Adapt" nor "Avoid" implies permission to trace; design our own assets.

Link pattern: https://citytransportmanager.com/presskit/files-screens/{iPad|iPhone|UI-English}/<name>.png

## City close-ups

**iPad/ctm-london-03-doppeldecker-nah** -- https://citytransportmanager.com/presskit/files-screens/iPad/ctm-london-03-doppeldecker-nah.png
Close 3D street grid with POI plates, stop pins, a bus label, green park at lower right.
- Adapt: white semi-opaque plates for POI names; flat-shaded extrusions with a lighter top and two wall tones; warm-tinted minority buildings; ghosted (faded) pins for unserved destinations.
- Avoid copying: the exact pin silhouette and colours; the "H" stop sign; real London POI names.

**iPad/ctm-hamburg-04-bus-nah** -- https://citytransportmanager.com/presskit/files-screens/iPad/ctm-hamburg-04-bus-nah.png
Bus close-up on a city street (inspected by lead only).
- Adapt: real-scale vehicle with tiny label plate and capacity bar.
- Avoid copying: their bus livery and model.

**iPad/ctm-hamburg-05-bus-nah-2** -- https://citytransportmanager.com/presskit/files-screens/iPad/ctm-hamburg-05-bus-nah-2.png
Second bus close-up (lead only).

- Avoid copying: their label wording format "B . 20 / 13/145" verbatim.

**iPad/ctm-aichhalden-tram-ins-gruene** -- https://citytransportmanager.com/presskit/files-screens/iPad/ctm-aichhalden-tram-ins-gruene.png

**iPhone/ctm-muenchen-03-tram-nah** -- https://citytransportmanager.com/presskit/files-screens/iPhone/ctm-muenchen-03-tram-nah.png

## Branding

**Logo files** -- https://citytransportmanager.com/presskit/ (files-logo/)
Not inspected by anyone; listed for completeness.
- Adapt: nothing.
- Avoid copying: logo, name, app icon, store badges, the "H" sign, "U" sign, pin forms, card collection, star currency, and any Rubik-like wordmark treatment that resembles their identity. Use our own name and mark.

## Cross-cutting avoid list
- The German "H" stop sign and "U" disc.
- Their teardrop pins with category icons in near-identical colours.
- Collector cards and star currency.
- Reusing their screenshots, map data renders or UI layouts one-to-one.
- Note: our map data (OpenStreetMap-style) must carry its own attribution.
